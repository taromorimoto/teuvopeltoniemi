Participation in a three-day visit by Finnish journalists to Strasbourg. The programme, organised and supported by the European Parliament’s Finland Office, included a meeting with Finnish MEPs, participation in the European Parliament’s October plenary session, an international journalism seminar, and the award ceremony for the Daphne Caruana Galizia Prize for Journalism. October 19–22, 2026.
