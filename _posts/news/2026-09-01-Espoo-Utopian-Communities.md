More than one hundred people attended my lecture on Finnish utopian communities at a meeting of the Middle-Espoo Seniors. The presentation generated a lively discussion, and many participants expressed interest in reading my book Paratiiseja rakentamassa (Building Paradises), which presents the history of Finnish utopian communities around the world. Espoo, September 1, 2026.
